Our Departments & Activities

Working across multiple fronts for community development and advocacy.

01. Foreign Affairs

Builds international partnerships and diplomatic engagement with global human rights bodies.

02. Programme

Planning and executing community welfare programs and strategic initiatives.

03. Finance

Managing financial planning, transparency, budgets, and resource allocation.

04. Community Engagement

Fostering strong community ties, social cohesion, and grassroots participation.

05. Education

Promoting learning opportunities, skill development, and educational support for youth.

06. Youth Empowerment

Empowering young leaders through training, workshops, and active participation.

07. Culture

Preserving and promoting Rohingya heritage, traditions, art, and cultural identity.

08. Refugee Affairs

Focuses on protection rights, legal aid awareness, and advocacy for refugee welfare.

09. Media

Managing digital communications, publications, and projecting community narratives.

10. Environment

Advocating for environmental awareness, sustainability, and green initiatives.
