Our Departments & Activities

Working across multiple fronts for community development and advocacy.

01. Foreign Affairs

Building international partnerships and diplomatic engagement with global human rights bodies.

02. Programme

Designing and executing community welfare programs and strategic initiatives.

03. Finance

Managing financial planning, transparency, budgets, and resource allocation.

04. Community Engagement

Fostering strong community ties, social cohesion, and grassroots participation.

05. Education

Promoting learning opportunities, skill development, and educational support for youth.

06. Youth Empowerment

Empowering young minds through leadership training, advocacy platforms, and capacity building.

07. Culture

Preserving and promoting Rohingya heritage, arts, literature, and cultural identity.

08. Refugee Affairs

Addressing core protection issues, human rights monitoring, and welfare support for refugees.

09. Media

Amplifying community stories, handling public relations, and managing digital advocacy content.

10. Environment

Advocating for environmental sustainability, green initiatives, and climate awareness in camps.
