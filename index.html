ABOUT RYAA

The Rohingya Youth Advocacy Alliance (RYAA) is a youth-led, non-profit, non-political, community-based organisation operating in the Rohingya refugee camps of Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh. RYAA is committed to empowering Rohingya youth and vulnerable community members through advocacy, education, humanitarian assistance, leadership development and community-centred initiatives.

RYAA seeks to strengthen community resilience, promote human dignity and social justice, advance the protection of human rights and create meaningful opportunities for youth participation and sustainable community development.

Our Vision

To contribute to a dignified, peaceful, and sustainable future in which the Rohingya community enjoys fundamental human rights, equitable access to education, and meaningful opportunities for sustainable social and economic development.

Our Mission

To empower Rohingya youth and vulnerable populations through advocacy, education, leadership development, humanitarian assistance and community-based initiatives that promote dignity, resilience and social justice.

Our Core Objectives

  1. Promote and protect human rights and uphold the dignity and fundamental rights of the Rohingya community.
  2. Empower Rohingya youth by strengthening leadership, skills, capacity, and participation.
  3. Promote access to education, learning opportunities, and mentorship.
  4. Provide humanitarian assistance to vulnerable individuals and families.
  5. Strengthen community participation, volunteerism, and social cohesion.
  6. Advance youth-led advocacy through dedicated awareness platforms.
  7. Preserve Rohingya culture, heritage, storytelling, and identity.
  8. Promote environmental protection, clean-up drives, and sanitation initiatives.
  9. Develop strategic partnerships with humanitarian agencies and development organisations.
  10. Ensure transparency, responsible financial management, and accountability.
